# Diagram Demo — For Software Analysts

Try the live demo: https://diagram-demo.onrender.com/

This demo is designed for software analysts who want to quickly create, explore, and share diagrams without dealing with technical details. The app turns plain-language ideas into clear visuals and supports the common diagram types analysts use every day.

**What You Can Do**
- **Explore Examples:** Browse ready-made diagrams to jumpstart your thinking.
- **Describe & Generate:** Type a plain-language description (for example, "Show the onboarding flow with steps A → B → C") and let the app generate a diagram automatically.
- **Live Preview:** See the diagram render instantly and iterate visually until it matches your mental model.
- **Export & Share:** Download SVG/PNG outputs to include in reports, presentations, or documentation.
- **Multiple Diagram Styles:** Create flowcharts, BPMN process maps, and architecture sketches from the same interface.

**Why Analysts Love It**
- **Fast visualisation:** Go from idea to diagram in seconds — no drawing skills required.
- **Consistent outputs:** Exports are publication-ready for reports and stakeholder decks.
- **Traceable changes:** Iterate visually and keep versions for review and sign-off.
- **Universal formats:** Exported images and files work with common office tools.

**Common Use Cases**
- Documenting workflows and business processes
- Mapping system interactions for requirements
- Creating handoffs and acceptance criteria visuals
- Preparing visuals for stakeholder meetings and reviews

**Quick walkthrough**
1. Open the demo: https://diagram-demo.onrender.com/
2. Click "New diagram" or open an example
3. Describe the process in a sentence and press "Generate"
4. Tweak the visual as needed and click "Export" to download an image
